# Copernicus

[中文](#中文) | [English](#english)

## 中文

本项目提倡数学评价的“哥白尼转向”：从追问“这项成果有多难、多新、多复杂”，转向追问“它为人的数学认识带来了什么”。

> **数学成果服务于数学共同体对数学对象、结构与问题的认识。**

- [哥白尼倡议](Copernicus.zh.md)
- [数学贡献的五维判据与五轴图](Assessment-Scheme.zh.md)

五维判据与五轴图是落实倡议的一种方案，可以随实践改进。评价的理由始终比等级与图形更重要。

相关项目：[MathMerit](https://github.com/GauSyu/MathMerit) 支持以人的认识为中心的人机协作数学评价。AI 阅读筛选是这一协作最直接的入口；人可以据此继续阅读、查证、讨论并修订评价。

## English

This project advocates a “Copernican turn” in mathematical assessment: from asking “How difficult, novel, or complex is this work?” to asking “What does it contribute to human mathematical knowledge and understanding?”

> **Mathematical contributions serve the mathematical community's knowledge and understanding of mathematical objects, structures, and questions.**

- [The Copernican Initiative](Copernicus.en.md)
- [Five-Dimensional Criteria and a Five-Axis Profile](Assessment-Scheme.en.md)

The five-dimensional criteria and profile offer one way to implement the initiative and can evolve with practice. The reasons for an assessment remain more important than its grades or diagram.

Related project: [MathMerit](https://github.com/GauSyu/MathMerit) supports human–AI mathematical assessment centered on human knowledge and understanding. AI-assisted reading selection is a direct entry point into this collaboration; people can continue by reading, checking, discussing, and revising the assessment.
