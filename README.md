## 中文

### 为什么做这个项目

随着 AI4Math 的发展，数学工作急速增加，“什么构成对数学的贡献”成了一个需要重新考察的问题。本项目尝试从以下第一性原理出发，探索一种数学工作的评价方法，供数学家们讨论、采用与指正。

> **数学成果服务于数学共同体对数学对象、结构与问题的认识。**

倡议正文见 [Copernicus 中文版](Copernicus.zh.md)或[英文版](Copernicus.en.md)。

相关项目：[MathMerit](https://github.com/GauSyu/MathMerit) 参考本倡议，提供 AI 辅助的数学文献阅读建议。

## English

### Why this project

As AI4Math develops, the volume of mathematical work is growing rapidly, prompting us to revisit the question of what constitutes a contribution to mathematics. This project takes the following first principle as a starting point to explore an approach to assessing mathematical work, which we invite mathematicians to discuss, adopt, and help refine.

> **Mathematical results serve the mathematical community’s understanding of mathematical objects, structures, and problems.**

Read the full proposal in [English](Copernicus.en.md) or [Chinese](Copernicus.zh.md).

Related project: [MathMerit](https://github.com/GauSyu/MathMerit) draws on this proposal to offer AI-assisted reading recommendations for mathematical literature.
